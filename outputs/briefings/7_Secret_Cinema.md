# Briefing: Secret Cinema

## Company overview

Secret Cinema is a UK-based immersive cinema and theatre producer, historically touring temporary venues, with around 500,000 annual visits across large-scale film experiences with sets, actors and themed bars. It is transitioning to permanent venues, having signed a 10-year lease at Greenwich Peninsula (opening with "Pirates of the Caribbean" next spring) and targeting a late-2026 East London venue for up to a decade. Merritt Baer now leads the company as part of TodayTix Group, recently acquired by MARI, alongside a returning hit run of Grease: The Immersive Movie Musical for summer 2026.
