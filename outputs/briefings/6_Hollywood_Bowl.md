# Briefing: Hollywood Bowl

## Company overview

Hollywood Bowl is the UK market leader in ten-pin bowling, operating 72 sites (part of a wider 77 UK and 16 Canadian estate) with roughly 14 million annual visits. H1 FY26 revenue rose 9.5% to £141.5m with EBITDA up 8.9%, and the group is mid-expansion — a new Edmonton centre just opened, Banbridge is under construction, and the pipeline targets 95 UK and 35 Canadian centres by the mid-2030s.
